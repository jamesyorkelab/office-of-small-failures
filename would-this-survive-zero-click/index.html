    What makes content survive zero-click search?

    Zero-click search happens when a person gets enough of an answer from a search result, AI answer or platform interface that they do not need to visit the underlying website. This tool assesses whether your page still gives them a reason to come.

    Information alone is easier to compress

    If the value of a page can be reproduced as a short answer, the visit becomes easier to skip. Pages become harder to replace when they include original evidence, interactive utility, distinctive experience, deeper exploration or something that changes over time.

    Utility gives the click a job

    A calculator, configurator, comparison, dataset, template or interactive tool can create value that is difficult to reproduce completely inside a summary. The visitor arrives because they need to do something, not just read an answer.

    Original evidence gives the page gravity

    Firsthand research, proprietary datasets, field experience and clearly attributable methods give other systems something to reference while preserving a reason to inspect the source.

    Return value matters

    A page that supports a recurring workflow, updates over time or stores useful work has a different relationship with its audience from a one-off informational article.

    Zero-click content FAQs

    What is zero-click search?

    It is when someone gets enough of an answer from a search result, AI answer or platform interface that they do not need to visit the website.

    Does a low score mean I should delete the page?

    No. It suggests the page may need a stronger reason to visit or a clearer role in the wider journey.

    Do I need to paste my content?

    No. The main assessment is entirely tap-based.

    Does this predict traffic loss?

    No. It assesses visit-worthiness under zero-click conditions; it does not forecast rankings, clicks or traffic.

    What should I improve first?

    Usually the weakest factor: original evidence, utility, depth, identity or return value.
