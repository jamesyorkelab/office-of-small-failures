How this AI visibility tracking tool works

Signal Sample is a manual AI visibility tracking tool for measuring sampled brand presence across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode and other AI-search surfaces.

Measure LLM visibility as sampled evidence

AI answers can vary by model, prompt wording, location, time and product mode. Signal Sample therefore records a defined sample: the question, surface, date, whether the brand appeared, whether a source was cited, which competitors appeared and whether the answer was accurate.

Track brand mentions, citations and competitors separately

A brand mention is not the same as a citation, and neither is the same as recommendation or accuracy. Keeping the observations separate makes the evidence easier to explain.

Repeat the same question panel over time

Keep a stable customer-question panel, run another observation round later and compare changes in presence, citation rate, competitors and accuracy problems.

Frequently asked questions about AI visibility tracking tools

What is an AI visibility tracking tool?

It records how often a brand appears in AI-generated answers, citations, competitors and changes over time.

Can you track ChatGPT rankings?

Signal Sample records observations rather than claiming ChatGPT has permanent rankings.

Which AI platforms can I record?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot and Other.

What is LLM visibility?

It describes whether and how a brand appears in answers generated by large language models and AI search products.

Does Signal Sample automatically query AI models?

No. The current version is manual and browser-only.
