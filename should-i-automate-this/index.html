Should this task be automated?

This free automation assessment helps you decide whether repetitive work is a good candidate for automation, should be assisted by AI or software, or should stay human.

What makes a task suitable for automation?

Good automation candidates are usually repetitive, frequent, rule-based, supported by digital inputs and reasonably reversible if something goes wrong. High-risk work, frequent exceptions and tasks where human judgement is the point need more caution.

Automation vs AI assistance

Not every repetitive task needs AI. Some work is better handled with a simple rule, workflow, spreadsheet formula or conventional automation. The tool separates the value of automation from the question of whether human judgement should remain in the loop.
