How this automation assessment tool works

This free automation assessment tool helps decide whether recurring work is a good candidate for automation, AI assistance or a human-led process. It scores repetition, rule clarity, exceptions, risk, reversibility, digital inputs and the importance of human judgement.

What makes a task suitable for automation

Good automation candidates are repetitive, frequent, rules-based, digitally accessible and easy to reverse when something goes wrong. Tasks with high risk, many exceptions or judgement at their core are usually better assisted rather than fully automated.

Automation vs AI assistance

Automation is strongest when inputs and rules are stable. AI assistance can be useful when the task contains language, summarisation or classification but still needs human review.

Assess the work before choosing software

Evaluate the process before choosing an agent, workflow platform or integration. A tool choice should follow the task design, not replace it.

Frequently asked questions about automation assessment tools

What is an automation assessment tool?

It evaluates whether a recurring task is suitable for full automation, partial assistance or continued human handling.

How do I know if a process should be automated?

Look for repetition, clear rules, low exception rates, reversible mistakes and digitally available inputs.

Should every repetitive task be automated?

No. Risk, judgement and the consequences of errors also matter.

What is automation vs AI assistance?

Automation is best for stable workflows; AI assistance is useful for ambiguous language or judgement-heavy steps with human review.

Does this build an automation?

No. It is an assessment and triage tool.
