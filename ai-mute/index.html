Free AI content filter

This tool is for people who want less AI discussion in what they read. Paste text from posts, newsletters or articles and it will flag and collapse sections that cross your chosen AI threshold.

How does the AI filter work?

It uses a transparent list of AI terms and basic hype signals rather than an AI model. You can choose Light, Normal or Nuclear filtering and edit the banned-word list yourself. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Does it automatically filter LinkedIn?

No. This standalone web version only filters text you paste into it. Automatic LinkedIn feed filtering requires the separate browser-extension version because one website cannot directly modify another website's page.
