Learn the maths

The Priority Desk works from incremental business outcomes. It does not turn issue counts, rankings, milliseconds or a generic “confidence” percentage into money.

Percentage points are not relative percentages

10.0% → 10.1% is a +0.10 percentage-point change and a +1% relative change. The journey model uses the absolute rate change.

Journey improvement

Outcomes = eligible exposure × absolute rate change × downstream conversion − adverse outcomes. Contribution = outcomes × contribution per outcome. Resource net = contribution − full resource cost.

Acquisition

Outcomes = net additional qualified visits × marginal conversion. If you bridge clicks to visits, compare with-change and without-change clicks, apply visits-per-click, then subtract displacement.

Failure recovery

Outcomes = eligible journeys × defect fraction × repaired fraction × outcome-probability gap. A break-even repaired fraction above 100% means the model cannot cover cost on those inputs.

Cash is not contribution is not capacity

Contribution gained, incremental cash spend and salaried capacity released are different viewpoints. Released time is not automatically cash saved.

A measured baseline describes an input. It does not validate an intervention effect. A conditional scenario means the arithmetic follows from assumptions. A measured effect needs a defensible retrospective design.

Commercial Jira ticket valuation without fake precision

The Priority Desk helps an in-house practitioner move through scope → value → challenge → choose → review. It accepts page-level, template, journey, sitewide and operational work, but only produces money when the entered mechanism and evidence support it.

Why some tickets should not get a pound value

Essential work can deserve capacity without a monetary forecast. Enabling or diagnostic work may need a cost cap and stop/continue condition. Where the effect is unsupported, the correct result is “benefit unknown”, a break-even hurdle, or a request for evidence — not an invented uplift.

How sprint comparison works

The comparison board keeps evidence status, working value, cost, engineering days and obligation status together. It checks capacity rather than multiplying money by a confidence score.

Privacy

The tool is static and client-side. It has no Jira connection, backend, external model call or analytics on this route. Manual entry is the primary route; exported JSON can be restored locally in the browser.
