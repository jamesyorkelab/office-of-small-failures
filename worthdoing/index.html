How this SEO prioritisation tool works

WorthDoing is a free SEO prioritisation tool for turning an SEO idea into a transparent commercial case. Add the traffic affected, expected uplift, conversion rate, value, confidence and delivery effort to compare likely opportunity with implementation cost.

Use an SEO ROI calculator without pretending the forecast is certain

The calculator estimates raw annual opportunity and then adjusts it for confidence and delivery dependency. It is designed for prioritisation, not to promise a precise future result.

What to include in an SEO business case

A useful SEO business case connects a proposed change to the audience it can affect, plausible uplift, conversion behaviour, commercial value, evidence strength and delivery effort.

When to use WorthDoing

Use it for technical SEO fixes, internal linking, landing-page improvements, content refreshes, template changes and other work where you can estimate the part of organic performance the task may influence.

Frequently asked questions about SEO prioritisation tools

What is an SEO prioritisation tool?

It helps compare SEO tasks using consistent factors such as likely impact, evidence, effort and business value.

Is WorthDoing an SEO ROI calculator?

Yes. It estimates annual commercial opportunity and adjusts it for confidence and delivery dependency.

Is the result an SEO forecast?

No. Treat it as a prioritisation estimate, not a guaranteed result.

What SEO tasks can I assess?

Technical fixes, content, internal linking, landing pages, template changes and other measurable SEO work.

Does WorthDoing send my data anywhere?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser.
