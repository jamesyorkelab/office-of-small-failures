How this AI content filter works

AI Mute is a free AI content filter for people who want less AI hype in the text they are reading. Paste posts, newsletters or article excerpts and the browser-based filter scores each section against editable AI-related terms.

Filter AI hype without pretending to detect every AI-written sentence

AI Mute is a keyword-and-pattern filter, not a forensic AI detector. That keeps the behaviour understandable and editable, but context still matters and false positives are possible.

Light, normal and nuclear filtering

Use Light to catch only heavily saturated passages, Normal for a balanced threshold, or Nuclear when you want almost any AI-heavy section flagged.

Why this is a paste-in tool

Browsers isolate websites from one another, so a standalone webpage cannot automatically hide posts inside LinkedIn, X or news sites. A browser extension would be needed for automatic feed filtering.

Frequently asked questions about AI content filters

What is an AI content filter?

It reduces or flags text that matches chosen AI-related terms or patterns.

Does AI Mute detect AI-generated content?

No. It filters AI-heavy language and does not claim to identify who wrote the text.

Can it hide AI posts on LinkedIn automatically?

No. This page only filters pasted text.

Does pasted text leave my browser?

No backend is required for the filtering.

Can I change the filtered words?

Yes. Edit the term list before running the filter.
